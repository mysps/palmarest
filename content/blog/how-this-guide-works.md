Palma has no shortage of places to eat and drink. It does have a shortage of honest, up-to-date guides that aren't three years old or quietly sponsored by the place they're recommending. This is our attempt to fix that.

## Where the places come from

Every spot on this site starts life as an Instagram account. We follow local venues, eat at them, drink at them, and decide whether they deserve a listing. If a place is here, it's because someone actually went, liked it, and thought you should know about it too — not because they paid to be listed or handed us a free bottle of wine (though we wouldn't say no to the wine).

Before anything goes live we confirm it's a real place you can walk into today, pin it to the right neighbourhood, and write a description that tells you something useful. No stock phrases, no "vibrant atmosphere" — just what the place actually is.

## Finding somewhere in under a minute

- **Pick a mood.** Hit *Tapas bar*, *Craft beer* or *Mediterranean* on the home page and the list narrows instantly.
- **Roll the dice.** Tap *Surprise me* and commit to wherever it sends you. It's more fun than it sounds.
- **Browse the map.** Every pin drops on the actual street. Pink pins are in Palma, teal pins are elsewhere on the island.
- **Use the search.** Type a neighbourhood, a craving or a street name and the list updates as you type.

## What you won't find here

No star ratings, no "top 10" lists that never change, no paid placements. Hours, photos and bookings live on each venue's own Google Maps page and Instagram — we link you straight there rather than trying to replicate information that goes stale the moment someone changes their Tuesday hours.

If a place closes, we remove it. If something is wrong, [let us know](https://www.instagram.com/restaurantsinpalma/) and we'll fix it.
